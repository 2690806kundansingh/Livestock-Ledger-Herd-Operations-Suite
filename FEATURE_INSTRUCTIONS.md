## 1. Government Subsidies & Bankable DPR Generator
*Route: `/subsidy-schemes`*

### Overview
Helps farmers and agribusiness entrepreneurs obtain up to 50% capital subsidies under Central & State schemes (**NABARD**, **NLM**, **PMMSY**, **AHIDF**) and generate verified Detailed Project Reports (DPR) formatted for bank appraisal.

### How to Use:
1. **Explore Government Schemes:**
   - Tap on any scheme card to review maximum subsidy percentages, financial caps, eligible components, and nodal helpline numbers.
   - Click **"Official Portal"** to visit government portals directly (e.g. `nlm.udyamimitra.in`, `pmmsy.dof.gov.in`).
2. **Generate Bankable Detailed Project Reports (DPR):**
   - Click the **📑 2. Bankable DPR Generator** tab.
   - Select your species scale:
     - 🐄 **10 Dairy Cows (Gir/Sahiwal)** — Total Outlay: ₹11,10,000 | 25% Subsidy: ₹2,77,500
     - 🐐 **50+3 Goats (Barbari/Sirohi)** — Total Outlay: ₹7,75,000 | 50% Subsidy: ₹3,87,500
     - 🐟 **0.5 Acre Earthen Fish Pond** — Total Outlay: ₹3,45,000 | 40% Subsidy: ₹1,38,000
     - 🐔 **500 Desi Country Birds** — Total Outlay: ₹2,25,000 | 25% Subsidy: ₹56,250
     - 🐖 **15 Fattening Pigs / 3+1 Breeders** — Total Outlay: ₹4,21,000 | 50% Subsidy: ₹2,10,500
   - Inspect Schedule 1 (Itemized CapEx breakdown), Schedule 2 (Annual Cash Flows & DSCR ratio), and Schedule 3 (Technical Assumptions).
   - Tap **"Print / Save as PDF"** to print or export the official bank appraisal document.
3. **Pre-Loan Document Checklist:**
   - Click **✅ 3. Document & Eligibility Checklist** to verify land title (7/12 extract), water test reports, vendor quotations, and CIBIL score (>650).
4. **Liaison Support:**
   - Click **"Apply for Bank Subsidy Consultation"** to connect with local agricultural credit officers.

## 2. Daily Production & Milk / Egg / Meat Ledger
*Route: `/production-ledger`*

### Overview
Daily operational ledger for morning and evening milk collections, automatic dairy union payout calculations (Fat & SNF pricing), poultry egg crate yields, and fish sampling.

### How to Use:
1. **Log Milk Collection:**
   - Tap **"Record Morning / Evening Milk Collection"**.
   - Select session (*Morning* or *Evening*) and animal (*Cow* or *Goat*).
   - Enter collected volume in Liters, Fat %, and SNF %.
   - The app dynamically computes price per liter and total session earnings using standard dairy cooperative pricing:
     $$\text{Rate (₹/L)} = (\text{Fat} \times 6.8) + (\text{SNF} \times 4.2)$$
   - Submit the entry to update daily stats and historical collection logs.
2. **Track Poultry Egg Crates:**
   - Click **🥚 Poultry Egg Crates** to log daily good eggs, broken/cracked eggs, and marketable 30-egg crate counts.
3. **Monitor Fish Net Sampling:**
   - Click **🐟 Fish Net Sampling** to log cast-net sample size, average fingerling weight in grams, Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR), and estimated pond biomass.
4. **Export Settlement Slips:**
   - Tap **"Print Daily Settlement Slips"** to generate physical receipts for milk buyers.

## 3. AI Animal Health Diagnostic & First-Aid Triage
*Route: `/symptom-diagnostic`*

### Overview
Visual symptom checker and emergency triage guide providing life-saving first aid in the critical first 30 minutes before a veterinarian arrives.

### How to Use:
1. **Select Species:** Tap on Cow, Goat, Fish Pond, Country Poultry, or Yorkshire Pig.
2. **Search or Filter Symptoms:**
   - Filter by severity: **🔴 Critical Emergency** or **🟡 Moderate Concern**.
   - Or search by symptom keyword (e.g. *bloat*, *swollen udder*, *gasping*, *blisters*, *anemia*).
3. **Execute Golden-Hour First-Aid:**
   - Expand the condition card to review:
     - Recognized clinical symptoms.
     - Step-by-step immediate first aid (e.g., vegetable oil + hing drench for bloat, sodium percarbonate oxygen granules for fish hypoxia, Virkon-S for poultry).
     - **⚠️ What NOT To Do** warnings to prevent fatal handling mistakes.
     - Licensed veterinary protocol required.
4. **Audio Guidance:** Tap the speaker icon to listen to voice instructions in clear Hindi/English.
5. **SOS Emergency Dispatch:**
   - Tap **"SOS Dispatch"** on any critical condition.
   - Enter animal ear-tag ID and pen location.
   - Dispatch transmits pre-filled case details to on-call mobile veterinary units.
